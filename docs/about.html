About Photonics Playground

Photonics Playground is a free, hands-on introduction to integrated photonics. Every page is a live simulation that runs entirely in your browser, from how light is guided in a waveguide to the inverse design of photonic components.

Privacy

This website does not use cookies, advertising or personal tracking, and it has no user accounts. All simulations run locally on your device. Nothing you enter or simulate is sent anywhere.

To see how the site is used, we collect anonymous, cookieless visitor statistics with GoatCounter. For each page view it records the page, the referring website, browser, operating system, screen size and country, and, for a few buttons such as "Optimize" or "Take the tour", that they were pressed. It sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and builds no profiles, so visitors cannot be identified. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.

Your browser stores a few small settings on your own device (for example, whether you have already seen a guided tour). They never leave your device and you can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. To deliver the pages, GitHub processes technical access data such as your IP address in its server logs. See the GitHub privacy statement for details. Fonts are served from this website itself, so no requests go to third-party font services. Apart from the statistics above, no other third-party services are used.

For questions about your data, or to exercise your rights under the GDPR (access, correction, deletion, objection), contact ch.stockinger98@gmx.at. You can also file a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde).
