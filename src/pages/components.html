Component Workshop

Now that we know how light is guided in a waveguide, we can use it to manipulate light on a chip, much like lenses, mirrors and beam splitters manipulate light on an optical table. These five components split, combine, filter and couple light, and they are found on almost every photonic chip. Pick one, change its dimensions, and watch what the light does in a simulation.

Model and accuracy. All components except the grating coupler use a 2D top-view model at 1550 nm. Its core index of 2.85 is the effective index of a 220 nm silicon film on glass (1.44). Waveguides are 300 nm wide, the widest that stays single-mode in this model, and the MMI ports are 400 nm. The grating coupler is simulated in side view: 220 nm of silicon (3.48) on 2 µm of oxide, with the Gaussian mode of a fiber (5.2 µm waist) as the target. Every picture is a frequency-domain wave simulation running in your browser.
